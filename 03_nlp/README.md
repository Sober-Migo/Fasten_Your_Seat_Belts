## 🎯 Campaign log

| Campaign | Theme | Key encounters |
|----------|-------|----------------|
| **C1 — Signals in the Static** | Classification & vector spaces | **Boss:** Naive Bayes + logistic regression sentiment classifier. Add word-embedding geometry to see meaning in distance. |
| **C2 — Probability & Language** | Probabilistic language models | **Boss:** Viterbi POS tagging. Autocorrect, n-gram LMs, Word2Vec. |
| **C3 — The Listener** | Sequence models · neural NLP | **Boss:** Named Entity Recognition. RNN sentiment, LSTM language models, Siamese similarity networks. |
| **C4 — The Dialogue** | Attention & transformers | **Boss:** Neural machine translation with attention. Text summarization, question answering, chatbot. |

---

## ⚔️ Master boss — *"The Dialogue"*

Before transformers, building a system that could hold a conversation meant teaching it to *attend* — to look at the right word at the right time. The attention-based NMT and the chatbot capstone were the moment models went from understanding sentences to understanding *conversations*.
