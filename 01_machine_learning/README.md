## ⚔️ Boss fight — *"The Pilot's Checklist"*

The hardest lesson of Flight School was not a formula: **a model that fails is usually a plan that failed.** Choosing what to measure, which slice of data to trust, and what to ignore beats squeezing 0.3% more out of an optimizer. That habit carried through every zone after this one.
